About Shree Kishan Ji Milk Food

Dedicated to paneer that belongs in every kind of kitchen.

At Shree Kishan Ji Milk Food, we believe that quality dairy products are an essential part of healthy and delicious everyday meals. With a strong focus on freshness, taste and product quality, we are dedicated to offering premium-quality paneer for households, restaurants, hotels and food businesses.

We specialize in Classic Paneer and Low Fat Paneer, offering products that combine great taste, versatility and quality for different food preferences.

From traditional Indian recipes to modern culinary creations, our paneer is suitable for a wide variety of dishes. We focus on product consistency and a satisfying customer experience with every purchase.
