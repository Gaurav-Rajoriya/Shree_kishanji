Made for food businesses

One versatile ingredient.
More possibilities for your menu.

Paneer fits naturally into a wide range of food-service operations. From à la carte restaurant dishes to buffet counters, delivery menus and large-batch catering, Classic Paneer and Low-Fat Paneer give kitchens options for different recipes and customer preferences.

01
Dine-in · Buffet · À la carte

Hotels & Restaurants

Build familiar favourites and creative specials with paneer used across starters, main courses and buffet selections. It gives chefs an adaptable vegetarian ingredient for everyday service as well as curated menus.

Menu ideas

  • Paneer tikka and grilled starters
  • Paneer butter masala and kadai paneer
  • Shahi paneer, palak paneer and buffet gravies
  • Paneer wraps, rolls and appetisers
Useful forRestaurant kitchens, hotel kitchens, cafés and buffet operations.

Delivery · Meal boxes · Quick service

Cloud Kitchens & QSRs

Create menu options that work across meal formats. Paneer can be included in bowls, rolls, sandwiches and combo meals, helping kitchens develop vegetarian choices for delivery and quick-service menus.

Menu ideas

  • Paneer rice bowls and meal boxes
  • Paneer kathi rolls and wraps
  • Grilled paneer sandwiches and burgers
  • Salads and contemporary fusion dishes
Useful forCloud kitchens, takeaway counters, QSR brands and meal-prep services.

Events · Bulk meals · Food service

Caterers & Institutions

Paneer is a familiar vegetarian menu ingredient for event catering and institutional food service. It can be planned into a variety of dishes for functions, workplace meals, canteens and group dining.
